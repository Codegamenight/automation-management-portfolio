# KPI Framework – Automation Operations

## Purpose
Provide standardized, objective metrics to measure reliability, responsiveness, and operational effectiveness of automation systems.

---

## Core KPIs

### 1. Availability (%)
**Definition:** Percentage of time the automation system is available for production.  
**Formula:**  
Availability = (Planned Production Time − Downtime) / Planned Production Time × 100  
**Target:** ≥ 98%  
**Frequency:** Daily / Weekly  
**Owner:** Automation Manager

---

### 2. Mean Time to Repair (MTTR)
**Definition:** Average time to restore system after a failure.  
**Formula:**  
MTTR = Total Repair Time / Number of Incidents  
**Target:** ≤ 30 minutes (critical assets)  
**Frequency:** Weekly  
**Owner:** Maintenance Lead

---

### 3. Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)
**Definition:** Average operating time between failures.  
**Formula:**  
MTBF = Total Operating Time / Number of Failures  
**Target:** Increasing trend month-over-month  
**Frequency:** Monthly  
**Owner:** Reliability Engineer

---

### 4. Incident Volume (Count)
**Definition:** Number of automation-related incidents.  
**Target:** Downward trend  
**Frequency:** Weekly  
**Owner:** Operations Manager

---

### 5. Preventive Maintenance Compliance (%)
**Definition:** Percentage of planned PM tasks completed on time.  
**Formula:**  
PM Compliance = Completed PM Tasks / Planned PM Tasks × 100  
**Target:** ≥ 95%  
**Frequency:** Weekly  
**Owner:** Maintenance Lead

---

### 6. Repeat Incident Rate (%)
**Definition:** Percentage of incidents recurring within 30 days.  
**Target:** ≤ 10%  
**Frequency:** Monthly  
**Owner:** Automation Manager

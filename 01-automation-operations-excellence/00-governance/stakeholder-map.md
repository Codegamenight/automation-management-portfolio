# Stakeholder Map – Automation Operations Excellence Programme

## Purpose
Identify key stakeholders involved in automation operations and define their interests, influence, and engagement needs.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Engagement Strategy |
|------------|------|----------|-----------|---------------------|
| Operations Manager | Overall operations accountability | High | High | Weekly status updates, KPI reviews |
| Automation Manager | Automation system ownership | High | High | Daily coordination, decision authority |
| Maintenance Lead | PM and corrective maintenance execution | High | Medium | Weekly planning meetings |
| Automation Technicians | Day-to-day system support | Medium | Medium | Shift handovers, SOPs |
| Production Manager | Throughput and availability | High | Medium | Maintenance window alignment |
| HSE Lead | Safety compliance | High | Medium | Safety reviews and audits |
| IT / OT Support | Network and system availability | Medium | Medium | Incident coordination |
| Vendors / OEMs | Specialist support | Medium | Low | Escalation via defined SLAs |
| Senior Management | Performance and cost oversight | Medium | High | Monthly executive summaries |

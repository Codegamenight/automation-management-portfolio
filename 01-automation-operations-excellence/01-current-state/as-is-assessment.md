# As-Is Assessment – Automation Operations

## Overview
This section documents the current operating state of automation operations, focusing on maintenance practices, incident handling, performance visibility, and governance maturity.

## Operating Model (Current)
- Operations are shift-based with varying experience levels.
- Preventive maintenance exists but is inconsistently executed.
- Incident response is reactive and technician-driven.
- Knowledge transfer relies heavily on informal handovers.

## Maintenance Practices
- PM schedules are defined but not always adhered to.
- Checklists vary by technician and shift.
- Limited linkage between PM findings and long-term improvements.

## Incident Management
- Incidents are handled locally with ad-hoc escalation.
- Root cause analysis is inconsistent.
- Lessons learned are not systematically captured.

## Performance Visibility
- KPIs are tracked inconsistently or retrospectively.
- Limited real-time visibility for management.
- Data quality varies by source and shift.

## Governance & Controls
- Roles and responsibilities are not formally documented.
- Change control exists informally.
- Risk tracking is reactive.

## Key Constraints
- Time pressure from production
- Skill variability across shifts
- Limited structured reporting

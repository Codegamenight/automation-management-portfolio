# 90-Day Roadmap – Automation Operations Excellence

## Days 0–30
- Formalize governance (RACI, RAID)
- Roll out KPI framework
- Standardize PM checklists
- Train technicians on incident playbook

## Days 31–60
- Enforce PM compliance tracking
- Launch weekly KPI review cadence
- Implement on-call escalation rota
- Start improvement backlog execution

## Days 61–90
- Review KPI trends and adjust targets
- Audit spare parts policy
- Conduct post-incident reviews
- Present progress to senior management

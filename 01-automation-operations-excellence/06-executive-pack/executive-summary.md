# Executive Summary – Automation Operations Excellence Programme

## Context
Automation operations are critical to throughput, safety, and cost control. This programme establishes a standardized operating model to improve reliability, reduce unplanned downtime, and provide management visibility.

## Key Problems Addressed
- Reactive maintenance and incident handling
- Inconsistent escalation and root cause analysis
- Limited KPI transparency
- Knowledge dependency on individuals

## Solution Overview
- Risk-based preventive maintenance strategy
- Standardized incident management and escalation
- KPI framework with defined targets and ownership
- Continuous improvement backlog and change control

## Expected Benefits
- Reduced unplanned downtime
- Faster incident resolution
- Improved safety compliance
- Predictable maintenance execution
- Data-driven decision making

## Governance
- Clear roles and responsibilities (RACI)
- Active RAID management
- Structured change control

## Conclusion
This programme provides a scalable, repeatable foundation for automation operations excellence and supports long-term operational resilience.

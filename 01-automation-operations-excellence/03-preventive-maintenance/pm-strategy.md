# Preventive Maintenance Strategy – Automation Operations

## Purpose
Establish a structured, risk-based preventive maintenance (PM) approach to reduce unplanned downtime, extend asset life, and improve system reliability.

## Asset Criticality Classification
Assets are classified based on impact to safety, production, and recovery time.

### Criticality Levels
- **Critical (A):** Immediate production stop, safety risk, or long recovery time
- **Important (B):** Partial production impact, manageable recovery
- **Non-Critical (C):** Minimal impact, no safety risk

## PM Approach by Criticality
| Criticality | PM Frequency | Depth |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| A | Weekly / Monthly | Inspection + functional testing |
| B | Monthly / Quarterly | Inspection |
| C | Quarterly / Semi-Annual | Visual checks |

## PM Planning Principles
- PM tasks scheduled during approved maintenance windows
- PM compliance tracked as a KPI
- Findings fed into continuous improvement backlog
- PM scope reviewed quarterly

## Roles & Responsibilities
- Maintenance Lead: PM planning and compliance
- Technicians: PM execution and reporting
- Automation Manager: Strategy ownership and review

## Governance
- Deviations require approval
- Missed PM tasks logged and reviewed weekly

# Spare Parts Policy – Automation Operations

## Purpose
Ensure availability of critical spare parts to minimize downtime while controlling inventory cost.

## Spare Parts Classification
- **Critical:** Single-point-of-failure components
- **Important:** Components with moderate recovery impact
- **Non-Critical:** Readily available or low-impact items

## Stocking Rules
| Classification | Stock Level |
|---------------|------------|
| Critical | Min. 1–2 units on-site |
| Important | Min. 1 unit or short lead time |
| Non-Critical | Order on demand |

## Governance
- Quarterly review of spare parts usage
- Obsolete parts removed annually
- Inventory accuracy audited monthly

## Ownership
- Maintenance Lead: Stock management
- Procurement: Supplier coordination

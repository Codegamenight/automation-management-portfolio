# Preventive Maintenance Checklists (Sample)

## Conveyor System (Critical Asset)
- Visual inspection of belts and rollers
- Check motor temperature and noise
- Verify sensor alignment and cleanliness
- Inspect safety guards and E-stops
- Verify communication status

## Sorter System (Critical Asset)
- Inspect carriers and mechanical wear
- Verify actuator operation
- Check lubrication points
- Test emergency stop functionality
- Review fault logs

## Control Cabinets
- Check cabinet temperature and ventilation
- Inspect wiring and connectors
- Verify PLC status indicators
- Backup PLC programs (if applicable)

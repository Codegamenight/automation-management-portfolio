# Incident Management Playbook – Automation Operations

## Purpose
Provide a standardized approach for responding to automation incidents to minimize downtime, ensure safety, and enable structured learning.

## Incident Severity Levels

| Severity | Description | Response Target |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| P1 – Critical | Full production stop or safety risk | Immediate |
| P2 – High | Partial production impact | < 30 minutes |
| P3 – Medium | Localized impact, workaround available | < 2 hours |
| P4 – Low | No immediate production impact | Next business day |

## Incident Response Flow
1. Incident detected
2. Technician performs initial assessment
3. Severity classified
4. Escalation initiated if required
5. Corrective action applied
6. System validated and returned to service
7. Incident logged
8. Post-incident review scheduled (if applicable)

## Roles
- Technician: First response and logging
- Automation Manager: Escalation and decision-making
- Operations Manager: Communication and prioritization

## Communication
- P1/P2 incidents communicated immediately to operations
- Status updates every 30 minutes for P1 incidents

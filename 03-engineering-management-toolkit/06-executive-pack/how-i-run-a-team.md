# How I Run a Team (Operating Model)

## 1 I set direction and protect focus
- Define clear goals tied to operational outcomes (reliability, safety, delivery)
- Align priorities with stakeholders and capacity
- Remove distractions and protect the team from unnecessary churn

## 2 I run a predictable cadence
- Weekly planning and review
- Daily short check-ins (or shift handovers)
- Weekly risk review
- Monthly performance and KPI review

## 3 I enforce clarity and ownership
- Every task has an owner, acceptance criteria, and evidence
- I expect early escalation when risk increases
- I drive standardization for repeatable work (SOPs, runbooks)

## 4 I lead calmly during incidents
- Prioritize safety and stabilization
- Assign a single incident lead
- Communicate facts, not assumptions
- Ensure postmortems produce actionable improvements

## 5 I manage performance with evidence and coaching
- Weekly 1:1s focused on priorities and growth
- Clear expectations and feedback with examples
- Recognition through increased responsibility, not vague praise

## 6 I build a culture of continuous improvement
- Treat recurring issues as system failures, not people failures
- Maintain an improvement backlog with visible priorities
- Track results using KPI trends

## 7 I communicate in a way executives trust
- Concise updates with RAG status, risks, decisions needed
- Metrics-based progress and transparent trade-offs
- Clear recommendations, not just problems

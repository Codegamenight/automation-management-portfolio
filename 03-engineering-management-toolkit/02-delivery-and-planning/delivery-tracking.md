# Delivery Tracking (Simple Operating Control)

## Purpose
Track delivery predictability without heavy bureaucracy.

## Weekly Delivery Metrics
- Planned vs Completed (%)
- Blockers count and aging
- Unplanned work (% of sprint)
- Incidents impacting delivery (count)
- Carry-over items (count)

## Management Actions (When Metrics Degrade)
- Reduce WIP and clarify priorities
- Rebalance capacity (ops vs improvement work)
- Increase quality gates (to reduce rework)
- Escalate dependency issues early

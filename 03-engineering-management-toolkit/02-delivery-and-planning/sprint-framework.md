# Sprint Framework (Lightweight, Repeatable)

## Purpose
Create predictable delivery through a simple cadence, clear priorities, and measurable outcomes.

## Cadence (Weekly Sprint Recommended)
- Sprint Planning: 30–45 minutes (start of week)
- Daily Check-in: 10 minutes (shift handover or stand-up)
- Mid-week Risk Check: 15 minutes (scope/risk adjustments)
- Sprint Review: 30 minutes (end of week)
- Retro: 20 minutes (what to improve)

## Inputs
- Operational priorities (downtime trends, incident backlog)
- Maintenance requirements (PM plan and overdue tasks)
- Improvement backlog items (highest value, lowest risk)
- Stakeholder constraints (maintenance windows, production load)

## Outputs
- Sprint goal (1 sentence)
- Committed work items with acceptance criteria
- Identified risks (added to RAID if relevant)
- Owner per task
- Evidence of completion (logs, screenshots, measurements)

## Definition of Ready (DoR)
A task is ready when:
- Scope is clear
- Owner is assigned
- Acceptance criteria exist
- Dependencies are identified
- Risk is understood

## Definition of Done (DoD)
A task is done when:
- Work completed and validated
- Documentation updated (runbook/SOP if needed)
- Monitoring/alerts considered (if applicable)
- Stakeholders informed (if impact exists)
- Evidence captured

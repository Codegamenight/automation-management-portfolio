# Roles and Expectations

## Manager (Engineering/Automation Manager)
- Sets direction, priorities, and success metrics
- Ensures staffing, capability development, and performance management
- Owns stakeholder communication and escalation
- Removes blockers and protects focus
- Ensures governance: RAID, decision log, cadence

## Technical Lead / Senior Engineer
- Owns technical quality and standards
- Drives design decisions and reviews
- Coaches engineers/technicians
- Ensures operational readiness (runbooks, monitoring, rollback)

## Engineers / Technicians
- Execute tasks against agreed acceptance criteria
- Log work transparently (what was done, why, evidence)
- Escalate early when risk increases or scope changes
- Participate in postmortems and improvement actions

## Stakeholders (Ops/Production/IT)
- Provide requirements, constraints, and operational priorities
- Approve maintenance windows and business-impacting changes
- Participate in KPI reviews and prioritization when needed

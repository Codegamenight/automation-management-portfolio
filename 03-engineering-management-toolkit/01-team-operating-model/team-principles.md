# Team Principles (How We Operate)

1. Safety and stability first  
We protect people and operations. We do not trade safety or reliability for speed.

2. Ownership and accountability  
We own outcomes end-to-end. When something breaks, we fix it and we improve the system.

3. Clarity beats intensity  
Clear priorities, roles, and acceptance criteria prevent chaos. Busy is not progress.

4. Standardization enables scale  
We standardize repeatable work (SOPs, runbooks, templates) to reduce variance and increase quality.

5. Data-driven decisions  
We use KPIs, incident data, and trend analysis to prioritize and justify investments.

6. Fast feedback loops  
Small, frequent improvements are preferred over large, risky changes.

7. Respectful, direct communication  
We escalate early, communicate factually, and treat feedback as a tool for performance.

8. Continuous learning  
Every incident, maintenance action, and project is an opportunity to improve capability.

# Performance Management (Operating Approach)

## Goal
Build a high-performing technical team with clear expectations, measurable outcomes, and continuous coaching.

## Performance Dimensions
1. Reliability impact (downtime reduction, MTTR improvements, quality of fixes)
2. Delivery effectiveness (commitments met, predictable execution)
3. Operational discipline (documentation, handovers, safety, change control)
4. Collaboration (communication, escalation, stakeholder management)
5. Growth (learning, upskilling, mentoring others)

## Cadence
- Weekly 1:1s: priorities, blockers, development
- Monthly performance check: evidence-based review
- Quarterly goals: measurable outcomes aligned to business KPIs

## Coaching Model
- Clarify expectations (definition of done)
- Observe performance via evidence (tickets, logs, incidents, outcomes)
- Provide direct feedback with examples
- Agree improvement actions and timeframes
- Recognize high performance with expanded responsibility

## Handling Underperformance (Structured)
1. Identify specific gaps with examples
2. Agree a measurable improvement plan
3. Provide support (training, pairing, clarity)
4. Review progress weekly
5. Escalate formally only if sustained lack of improvement

# Decision Log (ADR-lite)

## Purpose
Create transparency and accountability for significant technical and operational decisions.

## When to Log a Decision
- Architectural or design choices
- Process or operating model changes
- Trade-offs impacting cost, risk, or delivery
- Deviations from standards

- Decision ID:

## Governance
- Decisions reviewed in sprint review or management forum
- High-impact decisions communicated to stakeholders

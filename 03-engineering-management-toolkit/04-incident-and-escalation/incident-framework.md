# Incident Framework – Leadership Perspective

## Purpose
Provide a clear leadership approach for managing incidents, maintaining control, and protecting teams and operations during failures.

## Leadership Objectives During Incidents
- Ensure safety and operational stability
- Restore service as quickly as possible
- Maintain clear communication
- Prevent blame-driven behavior
- Enable structured learning after recovery

## Incident Severity Levels
- Critical: Safety risk or full service outage
- High: Major degradation with business impact
- Medium: Localized impact with workaround
- Low: No immediate operational impact

## Leadership Responsibilities
- Confirm incident severity and priorities
- Assign a single incident lead
- Remove blockers and secure resources
- Manage stakeholder communication
- Decide when to escalate to vendors or leadership
- Ensure post-incident review is scheduled

## Communication Principles
- Facts over assumptions
- Regular status updates for high-severity incidents
- One communication channel to avoid noise
- Clear ownership of actions

## Transition to Recovery
- Validate system stability before closing
- Capture initial observations
- Schedule postmortem within 48 hours for critical incidents

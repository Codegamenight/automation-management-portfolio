# KPIs and Telemetry – Digital Twin

## Operational KPIs
- Throughput (units/hour)
- Availability (%)
- MTTR / MTBF
- Order cycle time
- Backlog and queue depth

## Equipment Health Telemetry
- Motor temperature
- Drive load
- Sensor fault rates
- Alarm frequency

## Analytics Use Cases
- Bottleneck identification
- Predictive maintenance indicators
- Capacity planning
- Scenario simulation (peak demand, failures)

## Visualization
- Real-time dashboards
- Trend analysis
- Heatmaps of failures and congestion

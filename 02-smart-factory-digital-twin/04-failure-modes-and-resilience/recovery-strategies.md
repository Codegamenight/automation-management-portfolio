# Recovery Strategies – Resilience Design

## Design Principles
- Fail safe before fail fast
- Controlled degradation over hard stops
- Clear ownership during recovery
- Data integrity preserved across restarts

## Recovery Strategies
- Redundant control servers (SCADA/WCS)
- Defined restart and re-synchronization procedures
- Manual override procedures with safety checks
- Predefined rerouting logic in WCS
- Post-recovery validation steps

## Digital Twin Role
- Simulate recovery sequences
- Validate restart procedures
- Measure recovery time (MTTR)
- Identify improvement opportunities

# Safety Considerations – Smart Factory Digital Twin

## Purpose
Ensure automation system design prioritizes personnel safety while maintaining operational efficiency.

## Safety Principles
- Safety over throughput
- Fail-safe design
- Clear separation of safety and control functions

## Safety Systems
- Emergency stops (E-stops)
- Safety PLCs and safety-rated I/O
- Light curtains, scanners, and interlocks

## Operational Safety
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures
- Safe access zones for maintenance
- Clear visual and audible alarms

## Digital Twin Role
- Simulate safety scenarios
- Validate emergency stop coverage
- Assess impact of layout or process changes on safety

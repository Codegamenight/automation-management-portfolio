# Smart Factory / Warehouse Digital Twin – One-Slide Architecture

Objective:
Provide visibility, resilience, and decision support for large-scale automated warehouse operations.

Architecture Layers:
- Physical & Control: Sensors, PLCs, drives, robots
- Supervisory: SCADA / HMI
- Control & Execution: WCS
- Execution & Planning: WMS / MES
- Enterprise: ERP

Core Elements:
- Event-driven data model
- KPI and telemetry layer
- Failure and recovery simulation
- Cybersecurity and safety controls

Outcomes:
- Predictable operations
- Faster incident recovery
- Improved capacity planning
- Strong alignment between automation and business strategy

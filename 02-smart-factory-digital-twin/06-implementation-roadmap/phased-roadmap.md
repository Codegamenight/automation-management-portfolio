# Phased Implementation Roadmap – Smart Factory Digital Twin

## Purpose
Provide a realistic, low-risk approach to implementing a digital twin capability in a live automated warehouse environment.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation (0–3 Months)
Focus: Visibility and data readiness

- Confirm system scope and architecture
- Establish event model and data sources
- Implement KPI definitions and basic dashboards
- Validate data accuracy and latency
- Train operations and engineering teams on interpretation

## Key Outcomes
- Single source of truth for operational KPIs
- Improved transparency of automation performance
- Early identification of bottlenecks and failure patterns

---

## Phase 2 — Stabilization (3–6 Months)
Focus: Reliability and resilience

- Integrate failure scenarios and recovery simulations
- Use digital twin to validate restart and recovery procedures
- Introduce predictive maintenance indicators
- Refine alerting and escalation thresholds

## Key Outcomes
- Reduced MTTR
- Improved incident response confidence
- Better preparedness for peak demand and failures

---

## Phase 3 — Optimization (6–12 Months)
Focus: Decision support and optimization

- Run scenario simulations (volume spikes, equipment outages)
- Optimize routing, sequencing, and capacity usage
- Support investment decisions with data-driven insights
- Integrate with broader enterprise planning tools

## Key Outcomes
- Increased throughput without additional hardware
- Improved ROI on automation assets
- Strong alignment between operations and business strategy

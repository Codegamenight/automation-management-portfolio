# Problem Statement

Highly automated operations generate large volumes of data, but performance issues persist due to fragmented visibility, reactive decision-making, and unclear prioritization of improvement efforts.

Common challenges include:
- Inconsistent KPI definitions across teams
- Delayed detection of performance degradation
- Repeated incidents without systemic resolution
- Improvement initiatives driven by anecdote rather than data
- Limited ability to demonstrate impact to leadership

This project addresses these challenges by establishing a structured, data-driven approach to operational improvement.

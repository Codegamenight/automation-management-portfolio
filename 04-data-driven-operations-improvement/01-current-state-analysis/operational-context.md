# Operational Context – Current State

## Environment Overview
The operation is a large-scale automated warehouse supporting high-volume inbound, storage, order fulfillment, and outbound processes. Automation availability and throughput are critical to meeting service-level agreements and customer demand.

## Automation Landscape
- Conveyor and sortation systems
- Automated storage and retrieval (AS/RS or AutoStore-style)
- PLC-controlled equipment with SCADA supervision
- WCS coordinating material flow
- WMS managing orders and inventory

## Operating Model
- Multi-shift operation (16–24 hours/day)
- Maintenance activities constrained by production schedules
- Incident response driven by on-call and shift teams
- Performance reviews conducted weekly and monthly

## Current Challenges
- Reactive response to performance degradation
- Limited correlation between incidents and long-term trends
- Improvement initiatives competing for limited capacity
- Inconsistent visibility of root causes across teams

## Opportunity
A structured, data-driven approach can shift the operation from reactive firefighting to proactive performance optimization.

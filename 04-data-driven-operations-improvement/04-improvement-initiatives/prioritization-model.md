# Prioritization Model – Improvement Initiatives

## Criteria
Initiatives are prioritized based on:
- Impact on availability and throughput
- Reduction in MTTR and incident recurrence
- Effort and implementation complexity
- Alignment with business priorities

## Scoring Approach
- Impact: High / Medium / Low
- Effort: High / Medium / Low
- Priority determined by high impact with manageable effort

## Initial Prioritization
1. Asset criticality classification
2. Standardized incident playbooks
3. Alarm rationalization
4. Preventive maintenance optimization
5. Integrated performance review

## Review Cadence
- Monthly review of backlog and priorities
- Adjust based on KPI trends and operational feedback

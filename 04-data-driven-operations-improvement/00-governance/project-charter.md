# Project Charter – Data-Driven Operations Improvement

## Purpose
Demonstrate how operational data can be systematically used to identify performance gaps, prioritize improvement initiatives, and deliver measurable business outcomes in a large-scale automation environment.

## Objectives
1. Establish a clear operational context and performance baseline.
2. Identify critical KPIs that reflect reliability, throughput, and efficiency.
3. Analyze data to uncover trends, bottlenecks, and root causes.
4. Define targeted improvement initiatives based on evidence.
5. Track impact through measurable KPI improvements.
6. Communicate results in an executive-ready format.

## Scope
In scope:
- Automation operations performance data
- Reliability and throughput metrics
- Maintenance and incident data
- Improvement prioritization and impact tracking

Out of scope:
- Live system deployment
- Vendor-specific tooling
- Financial system integration

## Success Criteria
- Clear linkage between data, insights, and actions
- Improvement initiatives aligned to operational priorities
- KPI-based measurement of impact
- Executive clarity and credibility

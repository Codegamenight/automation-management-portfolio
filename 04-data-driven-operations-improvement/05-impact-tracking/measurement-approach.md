# Measurement Approach – Tracking Impact

## Measurement Principles
- Use consistent KPI definitions
- Track trends, not single data points
- Correlate actions with outcomes
- Review regularly with stakeholders

## Data Collection
- Automated KPI extraction from control and execution systems
- Weekly KPI snapshots
- Monthly trend analysis

## Review Cadence
- Weekly operational review (short-term deviations)
- Monthly performance review (trend and root cause)
- Quarterly leadership review (strategic alignment)

## Governance
- KPI ownership assigned per metric
- Actions tracked against improvement backlog
- Results documented and communicated to leadership

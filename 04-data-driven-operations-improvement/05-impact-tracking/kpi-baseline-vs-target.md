# KPI Baseline vs Target – Impact Tracking

## Purpose
Define measurable performance improvements resulting from data-driven initiatives.

## Baseline (Illustrative)
- Availability: 96.5%
- MTTR: 62 minutes
- MTBF: 18 hours
- Repeat incident rate: High
- Preventive maintenance compliance: 72%

## Target (6–12 Months)
- Availability: ≥ 98.0%
- MTTR: ≤ 40 minutes
- MTBF: ≥ 30 hours
- Repeat incident rate: Reduced by 30–40%
- Preventive maintenance compliance: ≥ 90%

## Rationale
Targets reflect realistic improvements achievable through focused prioritization, standardized work, and improved visibility rather than major capital investment.

# Key Findings – Operational Insights

## Overview
Analysis of operational data highlights recurring patterns that explain performance variability and reliability issues.

## Finding 1: Availability Loss Is Concentrated
- A small subset of assets contributes disproportionately to downtime
- Repeat failures indicate unresolved systemic issues
- Availability losses correlate with peak demand periods

## Finding 2: MTTR Variability Is High
- Recovery time varies significantly by shift and asset type
- Delays often linked to diagnosis time rather than repair time
- Escalation paths are not consistently followed

## Finding 3: Preventive Maintenance Effectiveness Is Inconsistent
- Assets with high PM compliance show fewer repeat failures
- PM execution quality varies between shifts
- PM timing is often driven by availability rather than risk

## Finding 4: Alarm Noise Masks Real Issues
- High alarm volumes reduce operator effectiveness
- Critical alarms are not always clearly distinguished
- Alarm floods precede major incidents

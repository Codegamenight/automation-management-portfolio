# Data-Driven Operations Improvement – One-Slide Results

Objective:
Improve reliability and throughput using operational data.

Inputs:
- Automation events and alarms
- Maintenance records
- Throughput and availability KPIs

Insights:
- Downtime concentrated on critical assets
- High MTTR variability driven by process gaps
- Preventive maintenance effectiveness uneven

Actions:
- Asset criticality classification
- Standardized incident playbooks
- Alarm rationalization
- Optimized preventive maintenance

Impact:
- Availability ↑
- MTTR ↓
- Repeat incidents ↓
- Stronger operational control and predictability

# Core KPIs – Operations Improvement

## Reliability KPIs
- Availability (%)
- MTTR (Mean Time to Repair)
- MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures)

## Throughput KPIs
- Units processed per hour
- Order cycle time
- Peak vs average throughput

## Maintenance KPIs
- Planned vs unplanned maintenance ratio
- Repeat failure rate
- Preventive maintenance compliance

## Quality & Stability Indicators
- Alarm frequency
- Rework or exception rate
- Incident recurrence

## Leadership Use
These KPIs provide a fact-based view of operational health and enable prioritization of improvement initiatives.
